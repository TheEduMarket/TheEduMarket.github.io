A few things you might be wondering

Good questions welcome.

What can I ask a student?

Anything about their experience: applying, choosing a programme, classes, campus culture, housing, or the everyday details you can’t get from a course page.

Do I need to prepare?

No prep needed. Bring a list of questions if you have one, or simply start with what you’re curious about.

Who are the student hosts?

Student hosts are verified before joining, so you can have a more confident conversation with someone who’s currently studying at the university you’re exploring.

Is this admissions coaching?

No. It’s a chance to hear a student’s personal experience and perspective—not professional admissions advice or a promise of admission.
